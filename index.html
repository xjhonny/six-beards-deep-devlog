How it was made

Six Beards Deep is a game about directing a crew: you are one weak dwarf who makes copies of himself and gives them orders. It was made the same way it plays. One person, llericop, set the vision, played every build and decided what made the cut. A crew of AI tools did most of the work.

  • Claude Code (Anthropic), an AI coding agent, drove the Unreal Engine 5.8 editor through its built-in MCP server. It wrote the game's Blueprint logic (as text, compiled into Blueprint graphs by scripts), the cave generator, the materials, the HUD and menus, the narrator's script, the play-test scripts and the trailer edit, and it kept this log.
  • ChatGPT / Codex (OpenAI) made the 2D art: concept art, textures, UI and reference images.
  • Tripo3D turned those images into the 3D models. Blender, scripted, rigged them and made the animations.
  • ElevenLabs gave the narrator his voice (236 lines), made the 31 sound effects and the music.
